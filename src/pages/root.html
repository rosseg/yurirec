What Ratings Mean

Most are straightforward but a few I want to explain a little better:

uniqueness
Whether a manga stands out in the crowd. A manga about two office workers, or a manga between two high school students are going to get a low score, unless they aggressively commit to the concept and stand out.

intensity
Intensity implies emotional intensity. If I cry, laugh, feel tense, worry for the characters, etc., my intensity score goes up.

Length?

Length is subjective. For my purposes, 1-20 chapters is short, 20-60 is medium, 60+ is long. These metrics are skewed by how long chapters are. Girlfriend Manual is 80 chapters but you can read a chapter in half a minute. I'd call it medium, still.

Pairing/Gender Scores

I rate each item, per relationship, and score them. If the lesbianism is palpable, Lesbian 10. Confirmed trans major character, Trans 10. Lower scores usually mean unconfirmed or subtext lesbianism (or whichever identity is relevant). Generally they'll be listed as 10s. If a comic is early enough, it might not be a 10 yet, because you can't guarantee where the author is heading with it, and sometimes I trust it well enough.

What Gets Added?

Mangas (etc) that I enjoyed or I think is interesting or unique and worth sharing. If the story is ongoing, I'll add it if I intend on finishing it.
