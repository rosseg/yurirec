Ratings Explained

Some are obvious, some less so.

priestess' reverence
How much I, Yuri Priestess Roz, enjoyed it.

uniqueness
Whether a work of yuri does enough to stand out. If it's full of tropes without a unique spin on them, the score will be low.

intensity
How emotionally intense I think it is to read. Very stressful stories or tearjerkers will have high intensity, while drama-free stories will have low intensity.

Japanese Creator Names

Names of Japanese creators are written with their last names first, like how they are written in Japanese.

How I Determine Length

For manga etc., I compare to a baseline of the typical manga with ~30 page chapters. For those mangas, roughly speaking, I use short to mean less than 20 chapters, medium to mean 20-60 chapters, and long to mean over 60 chapters. If the chapters are short or long, I adjust how many chapters each length is.
For visual novels, when counting the length of all the episodes, I consider 5 hours or less to be short, 5-20 hours to be medium, and 20 or more hours to be long. For anime, I consider the equivalent of 1 or 2 movies to be short, one ~12 episode season to be medium, and the equivalent of 2+ seasons to be long.

Pairing/Gender Scores

How lesbian, trans, ace each story is. If the lesbianism is palpable, Lesbian 10. Confirmed trans lead character, Trans 10. Lower scores usually mean unconfirmed or subtext lesbianism (or whichever identity is relevant). If a comic is early enough, it might not be a 10 yet, because you can't guarantee where the author is heading with it, and sometimes I have a strong gut feeling.

What Gets Added?

Mangas etc. that I enjoyed and that I think is interesting or unique and worth sharing. Though if its pretty forgettable from it not being very unique, I'll probably leave it out even if I enjoyed it. If the story is ongoing, I'll add it if I intend on finishing it.

My Yuri Diary

June 7th, 2026: 2DK, G-pen, Alarm clock.

I was craving a slow burn and 2DK was recommended to me. The author name rang a bell, and it turns out that Ohsawa-sensei also wrote "Hello Melancholic!", a high school band yuri that didn't do much for me.
2DK, on the other hand, hit the spot for a good slow burn yuri. Extra points for having an adult cast. The plot had its fair share of tropes and felt too convenient at times, but the characters' internal struggles kept me interested.

June 9th, 2026: How Do We Relationship?

Wow, what to say? It's easily one of the best yuri mangas and one of the new core pillars of the genre. It really stands out for its realistic depiction of lesbian relationships. If I had to recommend only one yuri manga to someone new to the genre, it's HWDR.

June 10th, 2026: PaperTeeth!

Caught up to episode 170. Laugh-out-loud funny. It's not yuri yet but with my yuri goggles on, what I see gives me hope. Season 2 is almost finished releasing I believe, and then hiatus time.

June 11th, 2026: There is No Love Wishing Upon a Star

Just so well done. The emotions are visceral. The manga I have the most reverence for, I think. I liked it even better on this re-read. Peak.
