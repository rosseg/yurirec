June 7th, 2026: 2DK, G-pen, Alarm clock.
I was craving a slow burn and 2DK was recommended to me. 
The author name rang a bell, and it turns out that Ohsawa-sensei
also wrote "Hello Melancholic!", a high school band yuri that 
didn't do much for me. 
2DK, on the other hand, hit the spot for a good slow burn yuri.
Extra points for having an adult cast. The plot had its fair
share of tropes and felt too convenient at times, 
but the characters' internal struggles kept me interested.

June 9th, 2026: How Do We Relationship?
Wow, what to say? It's easily one of the best yuri mangas and 
one of the new core pillars of the genre. It really stands out for 
its realistic depiction of lesbian relationships. If I had to 
recommend only one yuri manga to someone new to the genre, it's HWDR.

June 10th, 2026: PaperTeeth!
Caught up to episode 170. Laugh-out-loud funny. It's not yuri yet but with my yuri goggles on, what I see gives me hope.
Season 2 is almost finished releasing I believe, and then hiatus time.
