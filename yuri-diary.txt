June 7th, 2026: 2DK, G-pen, Alarm clock.
I was craving a slow burn and 2DK was recommended to me. 
The author name rang a bell, and it turns out that Ohsawa-sensei
also wrote "Hello Melancholic!", a high school band yuri that 
didn't do much for me. 
2DK, on the other hand, hit the spot for a good slow burn yuri.
Extra points for having an adult cast. The plot had its fair
share of tropes and felt too convenient at times, 
but the characters' internal struggles kept me interested.
